What high -> ultra costs, and where
-----------------------------------
RT roughly doubles (6.1-7.5 -> 11.7-12.9 ms) with no resolution change.  Two passes own
it almost entirely:
  Refl  1.13-1.71 -> 3.60-3.77 ms.  ReflOpaqueHalfRes 1 -> 0 is 4x the reflection rays,
        and it lands as a flat ~2.3 ms on every route.  This is the single most expensive
        preset toggle in the engine.
  AO    1.71-2.30 -> 2.99-4.26 ms, from 4 -> 8 samples.  Not quite 2x, so the pass has
        meaningful fixed cost.
  FroxelFill 0.49-0.67 -> 1.15-1.47 ms tracks the froxel grid's 2.5x voxel count.
Reflection denoising does not scale: ReflTemporal and ReflAtrous are flat 0.18-0.40 ms
from med through ultra.  Denoise is ~5% of reflection cost at ultra.
gpu.TLAS is 0.15-0.17 ms in all 13 captures, as it should be for resolution-independent
work.  It was 0.22-0.25 on the 2070.

MEASUREMENT: the two frame-time estimates diverge on this card
--------------------------------------------------------------
On the 2070, CPU wall and gpu.total + gpu.raster agreed within 2-3% everywhere, so either
could be quoted.  Here they do not:
  ultra        wall 13.7-15.7 vs GPU sum 14.5-16.4.  Agree within 6%, frame.fenceWait p50
               9.0-11.3 ms.  Clean GPU-bound; trust these numbers.
  high         wall 6.0-8.8 vs GPU sum 8.2-9.7.  fenceWait p50 1.3-4.5 ms.  Mostly
               GPU-bound.
  med          wall 2.9-4.0 vs GPU sum 4.6-5.3, a 1.3-1.7x gap, and fenceWait p50 is
               0.00 — nothing blocks.  A GPU span cannot exceed the frame it sits in while
               nothing waits on it, so at med the GPU spans are inflated: the timestamps
               bracket barrier bubbles and clock ramp-up on a card that is 60% idle.
  The table above takes the per-frame max, which is a lower bound on frame time and an
  upper bound on GPU work.  At med it is neither tight nor interesting; med on this card
  is simply "far above any refresh rate you have".  At high and ultra it is accurate.
  Rule earned: check frame.fenceWait before trusting a GPU-span sum as frame time.  Zero
  fenceWait with a GPU sum above the frame means the spans are padded, not that the GPU
  is behind.

FINDING: at med and high the frame-time tail is the game tic, not the renderer.  Of the
  frames at or above the capture's own p95: delta_med 96% carry frame.game > 4 ms,
  r2r_med 91%, mchq_high 51%, commfire_high 18%, r2r_ultra 4%.  So the crossover is near
  high — below it the game tic sets the tail, above it the GPU does.
  The height of that tail tracks how big the game spikes are in the session, not the
  preset.  mchq low / med / high and r2r high all land at p95 10.8-11.4 ms with
  frame.game p95 7.8-9.0, while the r2r med re-run reads p95 7.40 ms with frame.game p95
  3.85 — same mechanism, smaller spikes, 135 fps instead of ~90.  So do not quote a fixed
  "~90 fps p95 ceiling": quote frame.game.
  This promotes the 2070 doc's open frame.game item.  On a fast card it is the limit, and
  at 4.8-12% of frames it is frequent enough to feel.

FINDING: the BLAS rebuild hitch stays closed on this card.  cpu.TLAS.BLAS max is
  0.10-0.43 ms across all 13 captures.
  wait.uploadFlush is p99 0.00-0.05 ms everywhere, with n~=1 outliers at delta_ultra
  18.67, r2r_ultra 15.06, delta_high 11.74 — the same GPU back-pressure
  signature as before, and absent on commfire and mchq entirely.
  No mid-frame image loads, spans close exactly, VRAM 2.35-2.69 GB of 15.5, RSS 158-344 MB.

FINDING: serial shadow dispatch confirms in all 13 captures but is cheap here.  0.07-0.21
  ms per serial light at med, 0.16-0.62 at high, 0.25-1.04 at ultra, against 0.20-1.93 on
  the 2070.  62-88% of frames still overflow the cap of 8, and r2r_med overflows on 83%
  of 10166 frames at 0.08 ms/light.  commfire_ultra is the worst
  (1.04 ms/light, gpu.raster p95 11.2 against p50 3.7).  Still the top raster item, but
  it no longer sets the frame on this card.

Outstanding
-----------
  frame.game's 3.9-9.8 ms p95, now the binding constraint at med and high.
  Opaque reflections at full resolution cost a flat ~2.3 ms: the only thing separating
  ultra from high worth optimising.  Half-res + a better upsample would likely give most
  of ultra's reflections at high's price.
  Decide whether ultra should keep ReflOpaqueHalfRes 0 given it is what pushes the heavy
  routes to 40-50 fps p95.
