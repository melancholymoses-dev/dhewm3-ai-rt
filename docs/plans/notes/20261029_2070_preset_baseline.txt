What the presets actually buy
-----------------------------
AO is the biggest single step: 2 -> 4 samples plus native resolution takes it 1.4-1.8 ->
6.3-8.9 ms, a 3.6-4.7x jump for one preset notch.  It is the top RT pass at high in all
four routes.  Reflections are the second lever: low's glass-only mode reads 0.00 where no
glass is in view (mchq, r2r) and 0.34-1.20 where there is (delta, commfire).
Raster barely moves with the preset (3.8 -> 8.0 ms) because Interactions scale with
pixels only; the preset does not change the light or surface count.
gpu.TLAS is flat at 0.22-0.25 ms in all 12 captures, as expected for resolution-
independent work.

Low clears 90 fps on all four routes and is no longer pacing-limited.  The 2026-10-28
"low reads GPU 8.6 but backend 14.9, fenceWait 12.1, something paces near 67 fps" is
gone: backend p50 is now 4.9-7.8 ms with fenceWait 2.9-5.3.
Medium is the 60 fps preset on this card (57-76 fps p50), high is 28-38.

FINDING: the BLAS rebuild hitch is closed.  cpu.TLAS.BLAS p50 0.04-0.09 ms, max 0.28 ms
  across all 12 captures, including frames carrying 15-20 rebuilds.  The 2026-10-13
  block recorded 10-130 ms single frames on the same work.  Pooled storage plus the
  non-blocking upload removed it; delta, which was the worst offender, now shows
  cpu.TLAS.BLAS max 0.21 ms.  Drop this from the outstanding list.

FINDING: serial shadow dispatch still dominates and gets worse with the preset.  The
  handoff (Interactions climbs while Shadows falls) confirms in all 12 captures.  Cost
  per serial light scales with the preset: 0.20-0.41 at low, 0.30-0.83 at med, 0.58-1.93
  at high.  60-89% of frames overflow the cap of 8.  delta_high is the extreme:
  Interactions +28.93 ms across 15 serial lights, and gpu.raster p95 28.1 against a p50
  of 7.8.  This is the one unresolved GPU item in the sweep.

FINDING: commfire remains the worst route and for the same reason as on every other
  card.  gpu.Interactions p95 21.6 / max 27.1 at high; gpu.raster p95 23.6.  AO 8.88
  and Shadows 7.87 are also the highest of the four.  28 fps p50 / 19 fps p95 at high.

Remaining tails, none of them CPU work
--------------------------------------
  Worst single frames are all wait.uploadFlush: mchq_high 37.5 ms, r2r_high 29.1,
  delta_med 20.3, delta_low 12.2, r2r_med 13.7.  p99 is 0.06-0.13 ms, i.e. n~=1 per
  capture, and per the 2026-10-12 correction this tag is the CPU parking on the upload
  fence while the GPU catches up, not upload work.  commfire and delta_high show max
  0.11 ms, so it is not a property of the route.
  frame.game p50 0.23-0.53 but p99 6.0-9.6 ms, in 8-12% of frames, clustered rather
  than periodic.  Not explained by this capture and not attributed to any span.  First
  thing to check on the next pass; suspect fixedTic catch-up.
  No mid-frame image loads, no pipeline compiles above 0.33 ms, no VRAM pressure
  (2.3-2.5 GB of 7.4), spans close exactly (|residual - frame.other| = 0) everywhere.
  RSS is 180-320 MB and drifts under 70 MB per capture, i.e. the ~800 MB reading in the
  10-28 block was the post-vid_restart footprint, not a steady state.

Outstanding after this sweep
----------------------------
  Serial shadow dispatch: raise or remove the batch cap.  delta_high is the test case.
  AO at high: 4 samples at native 1440p costs 6.3-8.9 ms on a 2070.  Either the samples
  or the AO resolution needs a cheaper path before high is usable on this class of card.
  frame.game's 6-10 ms p99.
  No like-for-like capture of this build exists on the 4070 Ti Super or the 9070 XT, so
  there is still no cross-card ratio for the current engine.
